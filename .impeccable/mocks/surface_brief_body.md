# Surface Brief: Pollito Landing & Official Home

## Scope & Visitor Mode
Surface: `public/index.html` (Landing page, Slack App Directory official URL, and installation gateway).
Mode: Persuade.

## Direction contract

### THESIS
Pollito refuses generic white corporate templates and disjointed web dashboards. It establishes a high-craft dark slate command studio where consensus-building feels immediate, tactile, and native to modern engineering teams.

### OWN-WORLD
Deep slate canvas (`#0F172A`) with subtle mathematical dot-matrix grid (`#1E293B`). Accents in yolk-amber (`#F59E0B`), electric indigo (`#6366F1`), and consensus emerald (`#10B981`). Monoline vector character design with surgical geometry and high-contrast typography.

### STORY
The visitor immediately sees that team voting does not require leaving Slack or paying punitive per-user fees. They test a live interactive poll in the hero, see quorum calculate in real time, and click "Add to Slack".

### FIRST VIEWPORT
Top navigation with Pollito mark and pill 'Add to Slack' CTA. Hero split: left side holds bold headline 'Decisions made together. Without leaving Slack.', subcopy, and flat $15/mo pledge. Right side holds the centerpiece illustration: Pollito in glasses hatching from egg, examining an interactive floating Slack poll card with live animated progress bars.

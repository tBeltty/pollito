Slash Command Quick Reference

/pollito

Opens the interactive modal builder to craft a full poll with custom options, quorum targets, and anonymity settings.

/pollito "Question" "Option 1" "Option 2"

Instantly posts a quick in-channel poll with default quorum settings.

/pollito close [poll-code]

Manually closes an active poll before quorum is reached. Only creators and workspace admins can execute this.

/pollito help

Displays an ephemeral reference guide visible only to you.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are polls public or anonymous?

You can create either. By default, polls are public: cast votes and team avatars are visible directly in the thread. If you need honest feedback on sensitive topics, simply check the "Anonymous Ballot" option when creating the poll to hide voter identities.

Can workspace admins see who voted in an anonymous poll?

No. In anonymous polls, whether you voted and what option you picked are stored completely separately. Nobody—not even channel admins or poll creators—can see which choice any individual made.

How does quorum auto-closing work?

When creating a poll, you can set a quorum target (e.g., 75% of channel members or a specific number of votes). As soon as the threshold is met, Pollito locks the options, marks the poll as completed, and triggers a celebratory summary update.

What permissions does Pollito request?

Pollito requests the minimum scopes required to operate:

  • commands: To handle /pollito slash commands.
  • chat:write: To post interactive poll cards in channels and threads.
  • channels:read & groups:read: To calculate active member counts for quorum percentages.
  • im:write: To send non-voters gentle, opt-out reminders before deadlines.

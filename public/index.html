What Pollito does

Built for tech leads, scrum masters, and remote teams that need a decision by Friday.

Close at quorum

Set a participation target from 51% to 100% of channel members. When enough people have voted, Pollito closes the poll and posts the winner, or the tie, in the thread. Add a deadline from 15 minutes to 7 days if you want one.

Everything inside Slack

Manage polls from the App Home tab: active polls, past results, and participation rates. No logins and no external dashboard. Export CSV from a modal and save templates for polls you run every week.

Public or anonymous

Public polls show who picked what. Anonymous polls use a double-urn ballot: the record that you voted and the option you chose live in separate tables with no link between them.

Nudges for non-voters

Send a private DM to the people who have not voted yet. Nobody sees who already did, and each poll allows one nudge every 4 hours.

Live results

The poll card updates in place with vote counts and progress bars. Updates are batched over about a second, so a burst of votes in an all-hands stays inside Slack’s rate limits.

Permissions you can read

Pollito posts polls, reads channel membership to calculate quorum, and reads message events to detect when a Huddle ends. Slack lists every scope on the install screen before you approve.

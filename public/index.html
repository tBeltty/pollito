Built for clear team consensus

Designed for engineering leads, scrum masters, and remote teams who need decisions resolved without thread clutter.

Workspace Decision Hub

Review active polls, consensus history, and participation rates directly in Slack. Export CSV summaries anytime without opening an external dashboard.

Public or Anonymous Modes

Run open polls where team votes and avatars stay visible, or switch on anonymous mode so teammates can vote without social pressure. Nobody can see anonymous choices, including channel admins.

Automatic Quorum Closing

Set a participation target like 75% of channel members. Pollito locks the vote the second that threshold is reached and announces the winning choice in-thread.

Targeted DM Reminders

Ping only teammates who have not voted yet. Private reminders close participation gaps without public thread callouts or tagging the whole channel.

Sub-Second Live Tallies

Votes update in real time during live standups and all-hands. Built-in message queues absorb sudden voting spikes without lag or dropped responses.

Minimal Bot Scopes

Pollito requires only the permissions needed to post polls and record clicks. It never requests message history, private channels, or calendar access, speeding up workspace IT approval.

Transparent, Flat-Rate Pricing

Pollito runs on a freemium model that is 100% free during launch, with a planned $15/month flat-rate Pro tier per workspace and enterprise options for advanced governance.
